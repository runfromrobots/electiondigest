01Race developments

Ohio · Senate

Trump rallies for Husted in Vandalia, predicts a "big surprise"

Trump stumped Sunday for Jon Husted (R) and governor candidate Vivek Ramaswamy, telling the crowd "we're going to win, and we're going to win big" despite polling that favors Democrats. He leaned on AI data centers as a selling point while Brown's campaign countered on the electricity costs they bring. The headwinds Husted is running against are concrete: Ohio gas prices are up 35% year over year to $4.16/gallon, and Canadian retaliatory tariffs are hitting roughly $3 billion in Ohio exports.

Al Jazeera, Oct 4 — aljazeera.com
Texas · Senate

National Democratic money finally shows up for Talarico

New FEC filings show the Senate Leadership Fund-aligned Texas PAC spent $131 million in just 17 days to rescue Ken Paxton (R) — $61.4 million of it in a single two-day stretch. Pro-Talarico groups are still being vastly outspent (Lone Star Rising has put in $13.9 million since January), but Senate Majority PAC, a national Democratic outside-money group, has now launched its own independent effort for Talarico — the first sign of serious national Democratic money entering a race Republicans have had to defend almost alone against GOP cash.

Axios, Oct 1 — axios.com

02Polling & forecasts

Ohio · Senate

New poll shows a dead heat — but treat it as pollster noise, not a swing

InsiderAdvantage (fielded Sept 28–29, 1,200 likely voters, ±2.83): Brown 44, Husted 43 — essentially tied, and notably tighter than the Marist and Fox News reads from the same window, both of which had Brown up 8. This is volatility between pollsters' methodologies, not evidence the race moved; Cook's rating for Ohio Senate hasn't changed.

InsiderAdvantage, pub. Oct 1
Generic ballot

Silver Bulletin average holds at D+9.0 — no new reading since Oct 2

Nate Silver's generic-ballot average sits at Democrats +9.0 as of October 2, up from D+6.7 entering September. A Monday post elsewhere restating "Democrats lead on the generic ballot" appears to be citing this same figure rather than a fresh one — no meaningful movement to report today.
