01Race developments

TX Senate: Talarico challenges Paxton to a Fox News debateTX

At the Texas Tribune Festival, Democratic nominee James Talarico challenged Ken Paxton to debate on Fox News with Bret Baier moderating, saying Paxton has "been running from accountability his whole life." Paxton's campaign has not responded to debate invitations since August 24, per WFAA reporting, while national Republican groups have spent more than $160 million on ads backing him.

Texas Tribune — Sept 24, 2026
GA Senate & Governor: new YouGov poll, GOP gets its first governor's-race lead logged hereGA

YouGov (field Sept 15–18, n=3,447 registered voters): Ossoff (D) leads Collins (R) 51–41 in the Senate race. In the governor's race, Republican Rick Jackson edges Democrat Keisha Lance Bottoms 45–44 — within the margin of error, and the first Republican lead this digest has logged in that race (a July poll had Bottoms up 52–47).

Fox 5 Atlanta — Sept 23, 2026
ME Senate: Collins takes her first lead in an independent pollGOP momentum

NYT/Portland Press Herald/Siena College (field Sept 15–22, n=619): Susan Collins (R) 49, Troy Jackson (D) 46, with 5% undecided — Collins' first lead in an independent survey since Jackson became the Democratic nominee, reversing a 2-point deficit from June. The poll also found 55% of Maine voters want Democrats to control the Senate, but only 6% call that their top issue, and Jackson runs 9 points behind the generic ballot.

Portland Press Herald — Sept 24, 2026
KY-06: Beshear makes a personal push to flip a Trump+15 seatDem momentum

Gov. Andy Beshear is putting real weight behind Democrat Zach Dembo against Trump-endorsed Republican Ralph Alvarado in this open, GOP-leaning seat: roughly $250,000 in PAC ad spending, joint fundraisers and campaign ads, and personal lobbying of DCCC leadership to add Dembo to the Red-to-Blue program. Trump carried the district by 15 points in 2024, but Beshear himself won it by about 20 points in his 2023 reelection — the case Beshear is making for crossover potential, and for his own 2028 profile.

CNN via KEYT — Sept 24, 2026
CA-40: Kim distances herself from Trump branding in GOP-vs-GOP raceCA

Redistricting put two Republican incumbents, Ken Calvert and Young Kim, into the same Orange County-area seat. With the general approaching, Kim has started stripping "America First" branding from her campaign materials.

Roll Call, "At the Races" — Sept 24, 2026
National GOP candidates pivot on Iran and immigrationNational

With gas and diesel prices high, Senate Majority Leader John Thune acknowledged voter fatigue with the Iran conflict; Senate candidates Ashley Hinson (IA-2) and Mike Rogers (MI) both called for ending it, despite earlier votes against related measures. On immigration, Kansas Sen. Roger Marshall urged closer coordination with local law enforcement as ICE activity increases, and former Maine Gov. Paul LePage distanced himself from enforcement tactics after a relative with valid work authorization faced deportation proceedings.
