01Race developments

MI Senate

Former VP Kamala Harris campaigned with Abdul El-Sayed (D) in Metro Detroit this week, joined by Sen. Elissa Slotkin and ex-Rep. Sandy Levin mobilizing Democratic turnout. On the GOP side, Trump and Vance had already rallied for Mike Rogers ahead of the primary, and Trump-aligned and Musk-linked super PACs continue heavy ad spending for him — though a local strategist quoted in the piece said Rogers is "hammering the airwaves" while his ground game lags El-Sayed's organizing. All camps are now calling for gas-tax suspensions as pump prices hit records.

NC / GA Senate · Strategy

Senate Republicans are debating whether to redirect money out of North Carolina and Georgia toward states they see as more winnable (Michigan, New Hampshire, Iowa, Ohio, Alaska), with one GOP senator telling Semafor "North Carolina and Georgia have consistently shown us behind." NRSC chair John Thune says there's "game left to play" in both states. Roy Cooper (D-NC) and Jon Ossoff (D-GA) both hold polling leads over their Republican opponents. This is a strategic signal, not a rating change — flagged here for context, not scored.

02Polling & forecasts

Forecast movement

Multiple aggregators moved toward Democrats overnight: Kalshi now prices Democrats' Senate-takeover odds at 61% (up from "neck and neck" framing days ago) and Polymarket at 65%. Piper Sandler's research note raised its own House odds for Democrats from 70% to 80% and Senate odds from 40% to 45%, citing seven Senate races within roughly 2.5 points in the RealClearPolitics average. RCP's generic-ballot average now shows Democrats +8.5. Reporting attributes the shift partly to $4.47/gallon gas prices, $6.52 diesel, the Iran conflict, and the administration's disputes with TV networks. These are aggregate probability and generic-ballot figures — informative, but they don't count toward the seat-change score below.
