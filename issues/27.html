MI SenateRogers and El-Sayed meet for their first debate as Trump escalates attacks on El-Sayed

Mike Rogers and Abdul El-Sayed held their first debate Thursday evening in Grand Rapids. Trump posted on Truth Social attacking El-Sayed by his first name "Mohammed" and writing that he "goes back to the days of the Jihad," calling him a "RADICAL Extremist, Terrorist Sympathizer." El-Sayed replied that the president knows his name because "he says it every day," and said Trump was using his name and faith to distract from his policies. El-Sayed is campaigning with Barack Obama and Gov. Gretchen Whitmer to unify Democrats after a primary he won by under a point; he trails Rogers roughly 2-to-1 in ad spending.
