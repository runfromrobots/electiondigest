01Race developments

OH SENATE (SPECIAL)
GOP-aligned super PACs — Senate Leadership Fund, Musk's America PAC, Americans for Prosperity Action, and Trump-linked "pop-up" groups Ohio Flyer PAC and Buckeyes PAC — poured $14M into Ohio ads in the past week (race total now ~$298M, likely the cycle's costliest) attacking Sherrod Brown (D) over trans-rights votes and framing him as DSA-aligned. Despite the spending, Brown now leads Jon Husted (R) by 3–5 points and has outraised him $38.6M to $14.3M.

02Polling & forecasts

Sabato's Crystal Ball's Sept 17 "Seven Weeks to Go" column made 8 House rating changes, nearly all toward Democrats: NJ-7 (Likely R → Lean D), MI-4 (Likely R → Toss-up), IA-1 and IA-2 (both Lean R → Toss-up), and CO-5 (Likely R → Lean R, a softer move). Four more GOP-held seats — AR-2, OH-10, NV-2, NY-21 — were flagged as newly vulnerable. Crystal Ball's House tally now stands at 210 D-leaning, 203 R-leaning, 22 toss-ups. The column cites a New York Times/Siena poll putting the generic ballot at D+8.5, part of what it calls "cycle-high" 7–9 point Democratic leads, with "little improvement in the Republicans' polling position."
