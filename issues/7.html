01Race developments

Senate · Iowa
Cook Political Report moves open Iowa seat to Toss-up

The race to succeed retiring Sen. Joni Ernst (R) shifted from Lean Republican to Toss-up. A new Emerson College poll has Rep. Ashley Hinson (R) at 50% to Josh Turek (D) at 45% — within the margin of error — with Libertarian Thomas Laehn at 1%. Democrats are treating Iowa as a real reach opportunity in a state Republicans have carried comfortably in recent cycles; Turek, a former Paralympian and state lawmaker, is running on anti-corruption and campaign-finance themes.

Iowa Public Radio · Sept 18, 2026
Senate · North Carolina
ECU poll: Cooper leads Whatley by 7, widens to 8 forcing a choice

An East Carolina University Center for Survey Research poll has Roy Cooper (D) leading Michael Whatley (R) 46%–39% among likely voters, 14% undecided; when undecideds are pushed to pick, Cooper's lead grows to 52%–44%. Cooper leads heavily, 58%–25%, among voters who name the economy and cost of living their top issue. Note: this poll was fielded Aug. 31–Sept. 3 and published Sept. 15 — a bit older than our usual 24–48 hour window, but it's the first appearance of hard NC Senate numbers in this digest, so it's included for the data point.

WCTI/ABC 12 · published Sept 15, 2026 (field dates Aug 31–Sept 3) · ±4.4 MOE
