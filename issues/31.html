01Race developments

Texas Senate — Talarico back on the trail, debunks "stress episode" rumor, challenges Paxton on debate
Democratic nominee James Talarico returned to campaigning Oct. 12 after nine days out with the flu. Conservative allies of Republican Ken Paxton had claimed he'd suffered a "stress-related episode" and was treated at an Ohio psychiatric center; the center said he was never a patient there. Talarico's campaign confirmed the flu diagnosis and called the claims a lie. Talarico used his return to press Paxton, who has not responded to a debate request in 85 days. He's slated for a CNN town hall tonight (Oct. 14).
