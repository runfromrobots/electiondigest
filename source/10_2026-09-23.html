🗳️ Race developments

Source: Sabato's Crystal Ball, "Rating Changes Toward Democrats Across the Board" (Sept 22); confirmed via Washington Examiner and campaign reporting

📊 Polling & forecasts

National: NPR/PBS News/Marist poll (fielded Sept 14–15, published Sept 23): Trump job approval 39%/59% disapprove, with 53% saying they "strongly" disapprove — the highest strong-disapproval share recorded since 2017. Registered voters prefer Democrats on the generic congressional ballot 53–41 (D+12). 45% say their vote is "against Trump" vs. 25% "for" him.

Source: NPR, Sept 23

Nate Silver's average has Trump approval at 39% and the generic ballot at D+7.6.

Source: Forbes, Sept 22

No new movement: Cook Political Report's ratings pages are unchanged since last checked — Senate (Sept 15), House (Sept 11), Governor (Sept 17). No new Cook rating changes to report today.

🔒 Election administration & security

A federal judge (Kymberly Evanson) dismissed the Trump administration's lawsuit seeking Washington state's full voter roll, including dates of birth, driver's license numbers, and partial Social Security numbers. The ruling called DOJ's legal theory "not persuasive." Reporting puts this at the 25th such DOJ voter-data lawsuit lost nationwide, with an appeals court also having ruled against the administration in a Michigan case.

Source: Washington State Standard, Sept 22

Still pending: The SAVE database case (DHS v. League of Women Voters) remains before the Supreme Court, fully briefed since Sept 15 with no ruling issued as of this morning; SCOTUSblog noted a decision "could come at any time." The injunction blocking DHS's use of the database stays in effect in the meantime. The separate USPS mail-ballot rule fight was already resolved by SCOTUS on Sept 15 (injunction left in place) and has seen no further movement since.
