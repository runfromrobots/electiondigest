03

Election administration & security

Factual, sourced reporting only. No speculative rigging or "stolen election" scoring — that isn't something this digest quantifies.

Virginia restores voting rights to most people with past felony convictions — A federal judge (John Gibney, King v. O'Bannon) ruled Virginia's felony disenfranchisement rule violates the 1870 Readmission Act, since it stripped voting rights for far more offenses than were felonies in 1870. Going forward, only murder and manslaughter convictions disqualify a voter; civil rights groups estimate this restores eligibility to roughly 90% of the ~250,000 Virginians previously barred. The change lands just before early voting opens Sept 19 and ahead of the Oct 23 registration deadline; a separate ballot measure on automatic restoration is also pending.

Court ruling
VPM · Sept 15, 2026

DHS voter-database case still pending at the Supreme Court — No ruling yet on the Trump administration's request to lift the injunction blocking its rebuilt SAVE citizenship-screening database. The League of Women Voters and EPIC filed their opposition brief Sept 15, arguing the database is "error-laden" and endangers voters' privacy. The lower-court injunction remains in effect for now, so the database stays blocked.
