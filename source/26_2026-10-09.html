01

Race developments

New York governor rally

President Trump headlines a midterm rally tonight at Syracuse's OnCenter for Republican gubernatorial nominee Bruce Blakeman, who is challenging Gov. Kathy Hochul. It's his campaign swing into a state Republicans rarely contest at this level. The visit lands right after the most recent independent poll: Marist, fielding October 1–4 and released October 7, has Hochul leading 53% to 42%, a result in line with her lead all summer.

Sources: Finger Lakes Daily News (Oct. 7) · Marist Poll (Oct. 7, fielded Oct. 1–4)

Texas governor poll

A YouGov poll of 3,622 likely voters (fielded Sept. 28–Oct. 5) has Democrat Gina Hinojosa at 48% to Gov. Greg Abbott's 47%, essentially a tie. It continues a tightening trend first flagged in this digest Oct. 8: no public poll since mid-September has shown Abbott ahead by more than 5 points, after he led comfortably all summer.

Source: Newsweek (published Oct. 7, updated Oct. 8)
