NY GovernorMarist cross-tabs on Hochul's lead show a 29-point gender gap and a lopsided enthusiasm gap

Blakeman's supporters report far higher enthusiasm than Hochul's (80% "very enthusiastic" vs. 52%), and 40% of voters statewide say their family finances worsened over the past year. A Siena poll has Hochul +9 and a Quinnipiac poll has her +19 — a wide enough spread among pollsters to watch.
