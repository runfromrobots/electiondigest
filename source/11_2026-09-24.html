Race developments

Kansas · Senate

Marshall's billing practices become a Cook-cited vulnerability

Cook Political Report analysts pointed to scrutiny of Sen. Roger Marshall's (R) billing practices from his years as a practicing OB-GYN as a factor opening up a race Republicans had considered safe, against Democrat Adam Hamilton.

Cook Political Report, via Denver Gazette · Sept 23, 2026

South Carolina · Senate

GOP privately uneasy about Graham's soft polling, turnout risk

Republican strategists cited by Cook flagged soft polling numbers and turnout concerns for incumbent Sen. Graham (R) against Democratic challenger Dr. Annie Andrews, in a seat the party has not had to defend seriously in decades.

Cook Political Report, via Denver Gazette · Sept 23, 2026

Georgia · Senate

Collins still lags Ossoff in money and profile despite Trump, Kemp backing

Rep. Mike Collins (R) continues to trail Sen. Jon Ossoff (D) in fundraising and statewide profile even with endorsements from President Trump and Gov. Kemp, per Cook's analysis. The two campaigns traded new attacks during separate stops in Georgia this week.

Denver Gazette; WCTV · Sept 23, 2026

New York · Governor

Hochul tops 50% for the first time this cycle

A new Siena College poll has Gov. Hochul at 50% to Republican Blakeman's 41% among likely voters — the first time she's cracked the 50% mark in a Siena survey this cycle, though her overall lead has edged down one point from a 10-point August reading.

Siena College, via ABC7 New York · Sept 23, 2026

Polling & forecasts

Rating change · Senate

Cook moves three Senate races toward Democrats

Cook Political Report shifted three ratings on Wednesday: Georgia (Lean D → Likely D), Kansas (Likely R → Lean R), and South Carolina (Solid R → Likely R). Analyst Jessica Taylor described a "very blue environment forming," citing voter frustration over gas prices, cost of living, and the Iran war.

Cook Political Report; Axios · Sept 23, 2026
