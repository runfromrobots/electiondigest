Race developments

GA Senate

Ossoff and Collins trade ethics and extremism charges in first debate

The two met for an hourlong, personal debate in Atlanta on Oct. 8. Ossoff pressed Collins on a House Ethics Committee probe and his son-in-law's neo-Nazi social media posts, and said Collins still believes the 2020 election was stolen; Collins didn't directly answer that charge and called Ossoff a "trust fund kid." The two also split on the Iran strikes and arms transfers to Israel. They meet again at an Atlanta Press Club debate Oct. 13, the same day Georgia's early voting opens; Election Day is Nov. 3.

Source: Atlanta Journal-Constitution, Oct. 8–9

TX Senate

Talarico's 12-day absence from the trail draws Paxton and Trump mockery

James Talarico's campaign says he's been recovering from the flu since disclosing the diagnosis Sept. 30; his last in-person Texas appearance was a Sept. 26 press conference. Paxton led "Where is James Talarico?" chants at a San Antonio rally, and Trump posted that Talarico was "MISSING IN ACTION" on Truth Social. A campaign source says Talarico attended a private NYC fundraiser Sept. 28; his campaign says he'll return for a CNN/Jubilee Media town hall in Fort Worth on Oct. 14.

Source: Scripps News (10News), Oct. 9

KS Senate

Betting markets and Democratic money managers both lean into Kansas

Senate Majority PAC, the Schumer-aligned Democratic outside group, says it's "watching really closely" the Kansas race alongside Florida, South Carolina, and Mississippi, though it hasn't yet booked ad time there. Separately, Kalshi's betting market shows Republican Roger Marshall's win probability sliding from 82.8% on Sept. 5 to 62% on Oct. 8, tracking Cook's Oct. 6 move of the race to Toss Up.

Sources: States Newsroom/Kansas Reflector, Oct. 8, Kalshi News, Oct. 8
