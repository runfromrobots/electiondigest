Election administration & security

GA voter rolls

A federal judge (Victoria Marie Calvert, a Biden appointee) dismissed the DOJ's second lawsuit this cycle seeking Georgia's unredacted voter rolls, ruling the Civil Rights Act doesn't preempt Georgia's voter-privacy law and that the state's redacted public list satisfies federal law. It's the DOJ's 26th straight loss in these suits across 30 states and D.C. — no federal court has yet ruled in the department's favor on this issue.
