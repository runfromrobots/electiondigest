Election administration & security

UpdateThreatening-letter campaign against election offices is broader than first reported

The nationwide wave of suspicious letters mailed to election and congressional offices — first reported Oct 15 with evacuations in Louisiana and Oklahoma and letters to three U.S. Senate offices — turns out to have reached more states than initial coverage captured. Follow-on reporting adds Minnesota (Sens. Amy Klobuchar and Tina Smith, Rep. Kelly Morrison, and Secretary of State Steve Simon all targeted; USPIS intercepted the letters before delivery) and Wyoming (a letter aimed at Secretary of State Chuck Gray, no powder, with the FBI telling state police it appears to be a hoax), on top of previously-named Arizona, Nebraska, Maryland, Mississippi, and South Dakota, where similar packages were intercepted or received. The FBI and U.S. Postal Inspection Service are investigating; no arrests have been reported.
